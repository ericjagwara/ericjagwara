👋🏽 Hey there! I’m **Eric Ofuono Jagwara** — a tech explorer who blends **AI, robotics, and creativity** into things that *actually* make life better.  
Born and based in **Kampala, Uganda**, I spend my days teaching machines to think, helping people understand AI, and finding new ways to make technology serve communities — not just impress them.

I lead the **Young AI Leaders Community – Kampala Hub (YAIL Kampala Hub)** under the ITU **AI for Good** initiative 🌍.  
I’m also a **Zindi Country Ambassador**, a **mentor at MICCAI 2025**, and **Operations Coordinator at Tufuna Technologies / TechBuzz Hub**, where we experiment with everything from **IoT sensors** to **chatbots that care**.

I like mixing **engineering, art, and community building** — because honestly, that’s where innovation gets exciting.

---

💡 I’ve built and worked on:
- 🧠 **Mirembe AI** — a gentle mental wellness chatbot powered by Gemini API  
- 🕵🏽 **Fraudify AI** — real-time fraud detection for banks & telecoms  
- 🧮 **Inflation Forecasting Mapping Study** — systematic research on ML vs traditional models (2014–2024)  
- 🧩 **Interpret-IOI** — reproduction of Indirect Object Identification with TransformerLens  
- 🏫 **UrithiBot** — AI learning partner for schools using the ACE curriculum  
- 🎨 **Talents for Wealth Initiative** — helping creatives protect and monetize intellectual property  

---

I write code in  
`Python | PyTorch | TensorFlow | Flask | React | Next.js | Node.js | Docker | Git | Linux | NVivo | MAXQDA | CAD | Arduino | ESP32`

but what I really love is connecting people, sharing knowledge, and **building Africa’s AI future** — one collaboration at a time 💚.
